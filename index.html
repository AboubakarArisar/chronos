Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no paywall. The code is open source under GPL 3.0.

How accurate are the borders?

They come from the open historical basemaps project. Ancient borders are approximate and the app shows which map year you are looking at. You can report errors to that project.

Where does the information come from?

Region and event details are summaries from Wikipedia and Wikidata, with a link to the full article.

Can I use recordings in my YouTube videos?

Yes. Clips have no watermark. Please credit Chronos and the data sources in your description.

Does it work on my laptop?

It runs in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on most laptops from the last five years. A quality setting helps older machines.

Does it work on phones?

You can explore on a phone. Recording and the full interface work best on a laptop.

Where is the music from?

Public domain and Creative Commons recordings of traditional instruments and period pieces, credited in the app. Where none exists, the app generates gentle music in that culture's scale.

Can I help?

Yes. The project is open source, so you can fix a border, add an event or translate the app.
