Privacy

Chronos has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. It is a set of static files that run in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (quality, volume, mute, whether you have seen the introduction) are kept in your browser's local storage.
  • Article summaries you have opened are cached in your browser so they load faster and work offline.
  • The app's files are cached by a service worker so the globe works offline after the first visit.
  • Recordings and screenshots are made in your browser and saved only where you choose to download them.

Requests the app makes

  • Its own files, from the site you opened it on.
  • When you open a region or an event while online, your browser asks English Wikipedia for that article's summary and picture. Wikipedia sees that request like any other visit. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Nothing else leaves your device. You can clear everything by clearing this site's data in your browser.
