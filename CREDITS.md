## Data and software

- **Historical borders**: 49 world maps, 3000 BC to 2010. historical-basemaps, André Ourednik and contributors. License: GPL-3.0. https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps
- **Events**: Dated, located battles, treaties, cities, disasters, expeditions, buildings and monuments. Wikidata. License: CC0. https://www.wikidata.org/
- **Article summaries and thumbnails**: Loaded live in region and event cards. English Wikipedia contributors. License: CC BY-SA 4.0. https://en.wikipedia.org/
- **Earth textures**: Blue Marble day, Black Marble night lights, clouds, relief and water masks. NASA imagery, via the three-globe and three.js example assets. License: NASA imagery is public domain; packages MIT. https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/
- **three.js**: 3D rendering library. three.js authors. License: MIT. https://threejs.org/
